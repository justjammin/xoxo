## Design Context

### Users
Team QA reviewers, agent/tool authors, and staff engineers evaluating headless Claude and Codex. They audit regressions, choose model configurations, and tune prompts or skills from reproducible evidence rather than demos.

### Brand Personality
Precise, tactile, calm. XOXO borrows the wit and editorial confidence of a signed dispatch without copying Gossip Girl assets or turning the product into a novelty.

### Aesthetic Direction
An editorial laboratory notebook: light paper-and-ink default with a complete dark option, aligned X/Y evidence lanes, restrained oxblood and cobalt accents, ruled measurement details, and deliberate asymmetry. Avoid neon AI palettes, glassmorphism, gradient text, generic card walls, ornamental charts, and monospace-as-personality.

### Design Principles
1. Evidence before verdict: every score links to transcript, assertion, diff, or artifact.
2. Comparison is the spine: X and Y remain aligned from configuration through outcome.
3. Motion explains state: GSAP is bounded, interruptible, and removed for reduced motion.
4. 3D is optional evidence navigation: deterministic, lazy, capped, and paired with a table.
5. Review under pressure: WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard-complete workflows, readable density, and portable reports.
